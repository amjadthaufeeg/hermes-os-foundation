# Incident Response

## States
Detected → Assessed → Contained → Recovered → Verified → Learned

## Immediate priorities
1. Protect users and data.
2. Stop further damage.
3. Restore safe service.
4. Preserve evidence.
5. Communicate clearly.

Hermes may coordinate diagnosis and recommend actions. Destructive operations, production-data changes, and major rollbacks require the approval defined by Governance OS.

Every incident produces a post-incident learning record and regression guard where applicable.
