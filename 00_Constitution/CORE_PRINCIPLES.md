# Core Principles

- Preserve approved behavior by default.
- Inspect before editing.
- Ask for product decisions; do not invent them.
- Separate presentation, interaction, business logic, data, and architecture changes.
- One task, one contract, one branch, one active writer.
- No opportunistic refactoring during bounded fixes.
- A reviewer reports findings before making corrections.
- No task is complete without evidence.
- Never use production data in previews unless explicitly approved and safely anonymized.
- Stop when scope, authority, or requirements are unclear.
