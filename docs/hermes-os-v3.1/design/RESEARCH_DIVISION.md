**Status:** HOS-2 Specification

## Purpose

The Research Division provides structured, evidence-based research to inform product, design, engineering, and commercial decisions. Research is READ-ONLY. Research findings inform decisions — they do not make them.

## Research Outputs

Every research brief must include:
- Source references with dates
- Key patterns identified
- Relevance to AVOA
- Recommendations (advisory only)
- Limitations and assumptions

Research outputs are stored in `docs/hermes-os-v3.1/research/`.

---

## Authority

- Research may inspect public and approved internal sources.
- Research may produce briefs with evidence and recommendations.
- Research may NOT: approve product decisions, change task scope, modify code, merge, deploy, or directly instruct builders.
- Research findings return to Hermes for evaluation.

**Research is evidence, not authority.**
