# Universal Stop Conditions

The active builder must stop and return to Hermes when:
- the contract is contradictory or incomplete;
- a locked decision may be affected;
- an unapproved file or system must change;
- production data, secrets, payments, or destructive operations are involved unexpectedly;
- the baseline is dirty or cannot be identified;
- automated checks fail for an unclear reason;
- two repair attempts fail;
- the requested outcome requires a broader redesign;
- the active branch or commit cannot be confirmed.

Stopping is a control success, not a task failure.
