# Confidence and Escalation

Confidence is a reasoned control signal, not a fabricated percentage.

Use bands:
- HIGH: requirements, authority, affected systems, and tests are clear.
- MEDIUM: bounded uncertainty exists; proceed only with additional review or checks.
- LOW: material uncertainty about intent, scope, architecture, data, or impact; stop and escalate.

Escalate when:
- knowledge sources conflict;
- no reliable baseline exists;
- risk classification is unclear;
- a locked decision may change;
- two repair cycles fail;
- production safety cannot be demonstrated.
