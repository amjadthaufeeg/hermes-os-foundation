# Audit Policy

Every material task must retain:
- request and task ID;
- approved contract and amendments;
- base and candidate commits;
- changed-file list;
- automated results;
- review findings and dispositions;
- preview reference;
- human approval;
- merge and deployment record;
- rollback method;
- learning record.

Do not rewrite historical records. Corrections should be appended with date and reason.
