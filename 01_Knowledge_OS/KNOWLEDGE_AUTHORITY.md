# Knowledge Authority

When information conflicts, use this order:

1. Amjad's explicit current approval or instruction.
2. Locked Decisions.
3. Feature Disposition Register.
4. Accepted Decision Ledger entries.
5. Current approved Product Specification.
6. Business Rules and Architecture Decisions.
7. Design System.
8. Approved current Change Contract, only where consistent with items above.
9. Current repository behavior.
10. Previous task records and archived documents.
11. Conversation history.
12. General model knowledge.

## Rules
- Lower levels never silently override higher levels.
- Conflicts must be surfaced with source names.
- Archived or superseded documents are historical evidence, not active authority.
- Missing information must be marked unknown; it must not be invented.
